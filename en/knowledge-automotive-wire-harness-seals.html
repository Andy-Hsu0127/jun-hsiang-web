1. Why Self-Lubricating Silicone is Essential for Harness Seals

In vehicle engine bays, chassis harnesses, and industrial automation equipment, connectors rely on miniature Single Wire Seals (SWS). Conventional rubber requires manual grease application, risking terminal contamination and high assembly drag. Self-lubricating silicone continuously bleeds a microscopic lubricating film, facilitating smooth wire insertion without external lubricants.

2. Three Core Manufacturing Capabilities

  1. Precision Multi-Cavity Tooling: High-cavity mold design with strict flash control on parting lines and micro core pins.
  2. Controlled Bleed-out Rate Formulation: Customized phenyl silicone oil content tuned for optimal insertion feel without oil pooling.
  3. Wide Temperature Range: Stable compression set and elastic recovery from -60°C to +200°C under severe vibration.

3. Applications & Product Types

  • Automotive Electronics: ECU harness plugs, sensor connectors, lighting seals, door harness grommets.
  • Industrial Machinery: Heavy equipment electrical connectors, outdoor power tool cable boots.
  • Energy & Telecom: Solar junction box seals, outdoor telecom enclosure cable plugs.
