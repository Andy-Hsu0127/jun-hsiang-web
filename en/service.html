LSR Injection Molding FAQ

Answers to your questions about Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) injection molding technology and manufacturing processes.

What is LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber injection molding?

LSR (Liquid Silicone Rubber) injection molding is an automated manufacturing process where two-part liquid silicone raw materials (Part A and Part B) are mixed precisely at a 1:1 ratio and injected into a heated mold for rapid vulcanization. It is particularly suitable for high-precision, complex-shaped medical devices, electronic components, and high-temperature resistant seals.

How does LSR injection molding differ from traditional solid silicone compression molding?

LSR injection molding utilizes a fully enclosed automated feeding system, eliminating risks of human contamination, with very short cycle times, micron-level precision, and minimal flash. Traditional compression molding requires manual weighing and placement of solid compound, leading to slower production cycles; it is more suitable for larger parts or projects with limited tooling budgets.

What are Jun-Hsiang's advantages in LSR overmolding and insert molding?

Jun-Hsiang Enterprise has over 30 years of mold development experience, achieving integrated overmolding processes such as silicone-to-aluminum, silicone-to-iron, and silicone-to-plastic bonding. Through professional surface treatment and precise mold venting design, we ensure a tight bond between the silicone and the metal/plastic substrates, preventing delamination and meeting IP68 waterproof standards.
