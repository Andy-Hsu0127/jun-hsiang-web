1. Why Choose Silicone Overmolding for Mechanical Parts?

Traditional multi-piece assemblies relying on adhesives or mechanical fasteners often suffer from high labor assembly costs, tolerance stacking, and long-term joint degradation under vibration or thermal cycling. Silicone overmolding cures elastomer directly onto substrate cores, achieving both mechanical interlocking and chemical cross-linking in a single operation.

2. Key Substrate Combinations

  • Silicone to Metal: Aluminum alloys, SUS304/SUS316 stainless steel, copper, and iron for vibration isolators, medical handles, and rugged electronics.
  • Silicone to Plastic: High heat-resistant polymers (PA66+GF, PC, PBT, PPS) for ergonomic housings, waterproof bezels, and wearable enclosures.

3. Three Critical Factors for Zero-Delamination Bonding

  1. Surface Degreasing & Primer Application: Substrates undergo strict degreasing and specialized silane primer coating to form robust covalent chemical bonds.
  2. Precision Insert Fixturing & Flash-Free Mold Design: Proprietary cavity locking prevents substrate displacement under high molding pressure, maintaining clean parting lines.
  3. Controlled Vulcanization Thermal Profile: Uniform cavity heating ensures optimal silicone cure while safeguarding plastic substrates against thermal distortion.
