1. Introduction: The Material Revolution in Precision Engineering

In modern electronic waterproof components, automotive sensor seals, medical respiratory masks, and wearable smart devices, traditional rubber materials and compression molding often fail to meet stringent dimensional tolerances and cleanliness requirements. Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) has emerged as the elastomer of choice due to its chemical inertness, biocompatibility, wide temperature tolerance, and highly automated injection molding capability.

With over 30 years of custom silicone manufacturing and tooling experience, Jun-Hsiang Enterprise Co., Ltd. operates state-of-the-art Chuan-Lien precision LSR injection machines and 2.5D optical projectors. This guide details LSR material properties, LIM processing advantages, and key industrial applications for engineers and procurement professionals.

2. What is Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR)? Chemistry & Curing Mechanism

LSR is a two-component (Component A & Component B) liquid thermosetting silicone elastomer with a siloxane backbone (-Si-O-Si-). Unlike solid HCR silicone cured with peroxide, LSR utilizes platinum-catalyzed addition curing.

  • Component A (Platinum Catalyst) & Component B (Crosslinker): Stored separately in closed drums to ensure indefinite shelf life prior to mixing.
  • 1:1 Metered Dosing System: Pumped in an exact 1:1 ratio via an automated dosing system into a static mixer.
  • No By-Product Addition Reaction: Rapid crosslinking occurs instantly under elevated mold temperatures (170°C–200°C) without producing volatile by-products, resulting in pristine purity and safety.

3. Five Key Physical Properties of LSR

  • 1. Wide Temperature Resilience (-50°C to +200°C+): Maintains flexibility and elastic recovery under extreme cold or high heat without embrittlement.
  • 2. Biocompatibility & Medical Grade (FDA Approved): Passes FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 and ISO 10993 tests, hypoallergenic, non-toxic, and ideal for medical and infant care.
  • 3. Ultra-Low Compression Set: Retains excellent sealing memory over long-term mechanical compression, preventing leakages.
  • 4. Electrical Insulation & Weather Resistance: High dielectric strength and superior resistance to UV, ozone, and outdoor weathering.
  • 5. Low Viscosity & High Flowability: Easily fills intricate, micro-scale, or thin-walled mold geometries under injection pressure.

4. Liquid Injection Molding (LIM) Process & Jun-Hsiang Facility

LSR molding operates on Liquid Injection Molding (LIM) principles, opposite to thermoplastic molding: cool liquid material is injected into a heated mold for rapid vulcanization.

  • Chuan-Lien LSR Injection Machines: Equipped with cold runner systems to prevent premature curing in distribution channels.
  • Vacuum Venting & Flashless Tooling: Custom mold evacuation channels eliminate micro-bubbles and achieve flash-free part edges.
  • 2.5D Optical Inspection & Automated QC: Integrated optical projectors ensure strict dimensional accuracy for O-rings and custom seals.

5. Target Applications & Multi-Material Overmolding

  • Medical Devices: CPAP masks, valve diaphragms, catheter connectors, baby bottle teats.
  • Automotive Electronics: IP68/IP69K single wire seals, connector grommets, vibration dampeners.
  • Consumer Electronics: Smartwatch gaskets, earbud tips, waterproof keypads.
  • Multi-Material Overmolding (LSR-to-Plastic/Metal): Direct injection over PA66, PC, or metals (iron/aluminum) creating primerless, high-strength physical and chemical bonds.

6. LSR Liquid Injection Molding vs. Solid Compression Molding

Feature LSR Liquid Injection (LIM) Solid Compression Molding
Raw Material Two-part low viscosity liquid (A/B) High viscosity solid gum (HCR)
Automation Fully automated 1:1 dosing, fast cure cycle Semi-automated, manual preform placement
Precision & Flash Ultra-high precision, flashless edges Medium precision, requires manual deflashing

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the fundamental difference between LSR and solid HCR silicone?

LSR is a two-component liquid silicone rubber processed via automated high-precision Liquid Injection Molding (LIM) with platinum addition curing. It offers higher flowability, rapid curing cycle, flashless molding, and superior biocompatibility compared to solid compression molding.

Why is LSR injection molding preferred for high-precision waterproof seals (IP68/IP69K)?

LSR features ultra-low viscosity and high flowability, perfectly filling microscopic mold cavities without air traps or flash. Combined with vacuum venting, it yields minimal shrinkage and exceptional sealing tolerances.

Is LSR injection tooling cost high? Is it suitable for medium-batch production?

While initial LSR cold-runner mold costs are higher than compression molds, the fast automated cycle time and elimination of manual trimming significantly reduce total unit cost in medium-to-high volume production.
