1. Challenges in Thick-Wall & Large-Weight LSR Molding

Most liquid silicone manufacturers focus solely on micro-parts under 20g. When molding thick-wall or large-capacity components, manufacturers face severe technical hurdles:

  • Internal Air Voids: Trapped air during large volume filling causing internal voids.
  • Core Under-Curing: Silicone's low thermal conductivity leading to unvulcanized soft cores.
  • Sink Marks & Shrinkage: Surface depressions during curing contraction without proper pack pressure.

2. Three Core Process Capabilities

  1. Vacuum-Assisted Venting Tooling: Eliminates air entrapment during thick cavity filling.
  2. Zoned Temperature Control: Uniform thermal penetration ensuring complete cross-linking.
  3. Precision A/B Metering & Shot Packing: Prevents sink marks and ensures high tear strength.

3. Applications & Product Types

  • Industrial Protective Gear: Heavy impact silicone covers, high-voltage isolators, equipment damping pads.
  • Healthcare & Medical: Thick-wall medical tool ergonomic grips, rehabilitation elasticity blocks.
  • Heavy Sealing: Large-diameter thick-wall flange gaskets, extreme-environment conduit seals.
