Solid Silicone Compression Molding Technology

A traditional and mature silicone molding technology, where solid silicone raw materials are heated and vulcanized in a mold using vacuum hot pressing. It is suitable for large-volume production and offers relatively low tooling costs, making it the preferred choice for mass production.

Our core capabilities focus on pure silicone/rubber products, overmolding, plastic-to-silicone bonding, metal-to-silicone bonding, and other diverse processes. Located in Xinzhuang District, New Taipei City, our factory offers professional, one-stop OEM/ODM manufacturing services for clients throughout Taiwan.

LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber Injection Molding Technology

To meet the market's high precision and quality requirements, Jun-Hsiang has introduced LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber injection molding technology. LSR technology is characterized by high precision, high automation, and excellent production efficiency.

This technology effectively addresses our clients' demands for high precision and is widely applicable to medical devices, electronic components, automotive parts, and other high-accuracy applications.

OEM / ODM Workflow SOP

Standard 7-Step Custom Tooling & OEM Process

From 2D/3D CAD DFM evaluation, in-house mold making to T1 sample inspection, we enforce rigorous SOP. Mass production starts strictly upon customer sign-off.

01 CAD & NDA

CAD Review & Mutual NDA

Accepting .STEP, .IGES, .DWG, and .PDF formats. Mutual NDAs are executed prior to proprietary drawing review to safeguard your intellectual property.

02 Material Selection

Material Formulation & Hardness

Recommending optimal Shore A hardness and compounds (self-lubricating, high-temp silicone, fluorosilicone, EPDM/NBR) tailored to your operational environment.

03 DFM Engineering

DFM & Parting Line Engineering

Evaluating parting lines, draft angles, venting grooves, and wall thickness uniformity to eliminate shrinkage voids, air traps, and tear risks early in design.

04 Tooling Fabrication

In-House Precision Tooling

In-house steel mold making with high-precision CNC / EDM cavity machining. Tight tolerance control ensures runner balance, anti-flash boundaries, and desired textures.

05 T1 Sampling & QC

T1 Sampling & 2.5D Dimension QC

Producing first-article T1 prototype parts. QC engineers perform 2.5D optical non-contact dimension verification, flash inspection, and durometer hardness testing.

06 Approval & Sign-off

Client Fit Validation & Sign-Off

Physical samples are dispatched for customer fit-and-function validation. Tooling fine-tuning is performed promptly if needed to secure final engineering approval.

07 Mass Production

ISO 9001 Mass Production & Delivery

Executed under ISO 9001:2015 standards, including in-process patrol inspections, post-curing baking, final lot sampling, and secure packaging for global shipment.

OEM / ODM FAQ

Custom Silicone OEM & Tooling Manufacturing FAQ

Key answers for procurement managers and mechanical engineers evaluating silicone OEM factories, mold prototyping, MOQ, and material certifications.

Q1: What CAD drawing formats are required for silicone tooling evaluation?

We support standard 2D and 3D engineering CAD formats, including STEP (.step / .stp), IGES (.iges / .igs), AutoCAD (.dwg / .dxf), and engineering PDF drawings. If you only have physical samples, we can also perform reverse engineering measurements and technical evaluation.

Q2: Do you support low-volume prototyping? What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Yes. Jun-Hsiang provides flexible prototyping and pilot-run options, from prototype tooling trials and pilot validation runs to high-volume automated mass production. Specific MOQ depends on part geometry, material grade, and cavity layout. Contact us directly for a case-by-case assessment.

Q3: How long does tooling fabrication and T1 sampling typically take?

Tooling lead time and T1 sampling schedule depend on structural complexity, undercut features, and number of mold cavities. Upon receiving complete drawings and confirming the DFM plan, our engineering team provides a milestone timeline to keep your project on schedule.

Q4: How do you ensure strong adhesion in silicone-to-metal or overmolding parts?

Bonding strength relies on substrate pre-treatment, primer formulation, mold preheat temperatures, and precision anti-flash parting lines. With over 30 years of tooling experience, Jun-Hsiang applies dedicated positioning fixtures and molding parameter controls for aluminum, stainless steel, iron, or engineering plastics to prevent delamination.

Q5: Can Jun-Hsiang execute a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)?

Yes. We prioritize client patent and design confidentiality. Prior to receiving 2D/3D CAD drawings or initiating project consultations, we readily sign a Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to protect your proprietary intellectual property.

Q6: What regulatory and compliance certifications do your materials meet?

Jun-Hsiang is ISO 9001:2015 certified. Depending on your project requirements, we source and mold materials compliant with FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 food-grade standards, European LFGB, USP Class VI medical biocompatibility, RoHS, and REACH regulations, accompanied by material test reports and certificates of analysis.
