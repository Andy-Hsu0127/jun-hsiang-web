About Us

About Jun-Hsiang

Founded in 1991, Jun-Hsiang is a premier custom silicone and rubber molding manufacturer in Taiwan, with over 30 years of tooling experience and expertise.

Jun-Hsiang Enterprise Co., Ltd. was established on August 1, 1991, as a domestic factory specialized in silicone and rubber custom molding. Our main services include Compression Molding, solid silicone molding, and LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber injection molding.

Our molded products span silicone keypads, waterproof seals, O-rings, overmolding, silicone-to-metal bonding, adhesive rubber pads/feet, silicone sheets, automotive parts, electronic components, medical silicone, shock-absorbing rubber, and specialty rubber formulations. Upholding professionalism, prompt quoting, and outstanding quality is our promise to clients!

History

Company History

1991

Chairman Mr. Qi-Long Xu founded Jun-Hsiang Enterprise in Xinzhuang, specializing in custom rubber manufacturing.

1993

Added vacuum compression molding machines, primarily producing various keypads and sealing gaskets.

1999

Due to rapid business growth, the factory was relocated to Xinshu Road, Xinzhuang District to expand production lines.

2001

Entered the sporting goods sector, mass-producing high-quality swim goggles, straps, and protective silicone gear.

2010

Successfully mass-produced medical-grade silicone keyboards, exporting to Europe and North America, entering international medical device supply chains.

2012

To meet the smartphone boom, we mass-produced precision mobile silicone/rubber components and dust-proof parts.

2016

Invested in R&D and mass production of key silicone and rubber seals for electric scooters, expanding into the new energy market.

2017

Obtained ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System certification, implementing high-standard quality control.

2018

Expanded the factory, introduced LSR liquid silicone injection molding technology, and added smart QC equipment such as a 2.5D projector, force-displacement tester, and life tester.

2020

Upgraded and expanded the factory, implementing automated processes and smart QC systems to continually boost competitiveness.

2024

Continued refining technology, accumulating over 1,000+ custom projects, serving renowned domestic and international corporations.

Services

Services & Technology

From compression molding to LSR injection, we provide a full range of silicone and rubber molding technical services.

Solid Silicone Compression Molding Technology

A traditional and mature silicone molding technology, where solid silicone raw materials are heated and vulcanized in a mold using vacuum hot pressing. It is suitable for large-volume production and offers relatively low tooling costs, making it the preferred choice for mass production.

Our core capabilities focus on pure silicone/rubber products, overmolding, plastic-to-silicone bonding, metal-to-silicone bonding, and other diverse processes. Located in Xinzhuang District, New Taipei City, our factory offers professional, one-stop OEM/ODM manufacturing services for clients throughout Taiwan.

LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber Injection Molding Technology

To meet the market's high precision and quality requirements, Jun-Hsiang has introduced LSR Liquid Silicone Rubber injection molding technology. LSR technology is characterized by high precision, high automation, and excellent production efficiency.

This technology effectively addresses our clients' demands for high precision and is widely applicable to medical devices, electronic components, automotive parts, and other high-accuracy applications.

LSR Liquid Silicone High Precision High Automation Medical Grade Electronic Components

LSR Technology Highlights:

  • Core Process: Dual-component automated metering and mixing injection.
  • High Precision: Micron-level precision mold venting and injection, with tolerances controlled within ±0.02mm.
  • Material Advantages: Non-toxic, resistant to extreme temperatures, complying with medical-grade (USP Class VI) and food-grade (FDA) standards.
  • Overmolding: Professional overmolding / silicone-to-metal bonding with plastics, metals, and copper parts.
