What is Self-Lubricating Silicone (Bleed-Out Rubber)?

Self-lubricating silicone, commonly referred to as "bleed-out silicone" or "self-lubricating rubber," is a specialized silicone elastomer formulated with pre-blended phenyl silicone oil or fluorosilicone oil within the High Consistency Rubber (HCR) base compound. Unlike standard silicone, which has a high coefficient of friction and a dry, tacky surface after curing, self-lubricating silicone gradually "bleeds" its internal silicone oil through the crosslinked polymer network to the surface after molding. This creates an ultra-thin, self-renewing lubricating film that eliminates the need for externally applied lubricants throughout the entire service life of the component.

The key advantage is the self-replenishing mechanism: as the surface lubricating film is consumed through friction or compression, fresh silicone oil from the interior continuously migrates outward to restore it. This ensures consistent low-friction performance without any maintenance intervention—a critical requirement in sealed automotive assemblies and sterile medical environments where re-lubrication is impossible or undesirable.

Self-Lubricating vs. Standard Silicone: Key Performance Differences

The decision to specify self-lubricating silicone hinges on whether the application involves repeated insertion/extraction, sliding contact, or environments where external re-lubrication is impractical. The table below summarizes the critical differences:

Parameter Standard Silicone Self-Lubricating Silicone
Surface Friction (µ) High (µ ≈ 0.5–1.2, tacky) Low (µ ≈ 0.1–0.3, smooth)
Lubrication Requirement Requires periodic external lubrication Self-replenishing; no external lubricant needed
Temperature Range -60°C to +200°C -60°C to +200°C (identical)
Contamination Risk None from material itself Minimal; controllable via low-VOC (D4/D6) formulation
Molding Process HCR Compression or LSR Injection HCR Compression Molding (Jun-Hsiang specialty)

HCR Compression Molding Process for Self-Lubricating Silicone

Jun-Hsiang Enterprise manufactures self-lubricating silicone components using High Consistency Rubber (HCR) compression molding. In the compounding stage, material engineers precisely blend the chosen silicone oil (phenyl silicone oil for general industrial/automotive use, or low-volatility fluorosilicone oil for medical and electronic applications) into the HCR base at a controlled concentration to achieve the target bleed-out rate.

During compression molding, the silicone compound is placed into precision steel tooling and cured under heat (typically 170°C) and pressure. The vulcanization reaction locks the silicone oil molecules uniformly into the three-dimensional crosslinked network. Post-cure, the oil migrates slowly and evenly through the network pores to the surface—ensuring consistent bleed-out across every part in every production batch.

  • Hardness range: Shore A 30 to 70, customizable to match required insertion force and sealing compression set.
  • Oil type selection: Phenyl silicone oil (general industrial/automotive); low-VOC fluorosilicone oil (medical, electronics).
  • Post-curing option: Secondary oven curing at 200°C / 4 hours available to minimize residual volatile siloxanes (D4/D6) for regulatory compliance.
  • Quality certification: ISO 9001 quality management system throughout the production process.

Core Applications: Automotive, Medical & Industrial

🚗 Automotive: Wire Harness Seals & Connector Grommets

Modern vehicles—especially electric vehicles—contain thousands of wire harness connectors. Standard silicone wire seals create high insertion resistance, slowing assembly lines and risking wire jacket abrasion or incomplete seating. Self-lubricating silicone wire seals allow effortless wire threading, reducing assembly time by 200–300% while eliminating grease contamination risks at contact interfaces. Key applications include: automotive wire seals (single and multi-wire), rubber grommets, ECU housing gaskets, and door seal strips.

🏥 Medical: Plunger Stoppers, Catheter Valves & Needle Seals

Medical devices cannot use externally applied chemical lubricants on product surfaces. Self-lubricating silicone seals—formulated with bioinert, non-toxic silicone oil—meet the requirements of syringe plunger stoppers, IV catheter valves, and needle-free connectors where smooth actuation without contamination is critical. With appropriate grade selection (silicone oil compliant with USP Class VI testing protocols), these materials deliver long-term lubrication performance in sterile environments.

⚙️ Industrial: Dynamic O-Rings & Pneumatic Seals

In pneumatic cylinders, hydraulic valves, and industrial robot joints, standard O-rings operating under dry conditions suffer rapid friction wear and permanent compression set. Self-lubricating silicone O-rings dramatically reduce dynamic seal startup friction and extend service life 3–5× compared to unlubricated standard silicone, while simultaneously reducing operational noise and vibration.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will the bleed-out oil contaminate adjacent electronic contacts or medical fluids?

The bleed-out quantity is precisely controlled through formulation engineering and is extremely small—only a micrometer-thin film forms at the contact interface. For automotive electronics and medical applications, Jun-Hsiang recommends low-volatility formulations with minimal D4/D6 siloxane content, ensuring no contamination of electronic connector contacts or IV fluids.

Can Jun-Hsiang customize the hardness and bleed-out rate?

Yes. Jun-Hsiang Enterprise's engineers can customize both the Shore A hardness (30–70) and the bleed-out rate based on your application's insertion force requirements, sealing compression ratios, and environmental conditions.
