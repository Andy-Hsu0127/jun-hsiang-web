Rubber Factory & OEM Manufacturer Procurement Guide: EPDM, NBR, Viton

Industrial Rubber Components, Anti-Vibration Pads and Rubber-to-Metal Bonding

1. Industrial Rubber Material Selection Guide

Selecting the right rubber factory and rubber OEM manufacturer involves formulating the ideal compound for your working environment:

  • NBR: Excellent oil and fuel resistance for seals, O-rings, and gaskets.
  • EPDM: Exceptional weather, ozone, and steam resistance for outdoor seals and solar gaskets.
  • Viton (FKM): High temperature (up to 250°C) and extreme chemical resistance for semiconductor and chemical processing equipment.
