1. Introduction: Why Choose Taiwan LSR Liquid Injection Suppliers?

Demand for high-precision silicone parts in automotive electronics, medical respiratory masks, UAV shock absorbers, and wearable devices continues to rise. LSR (Liquid Silicone Rubber) injection molding (LIM) offers superior fluidity, platinum-cure purity, automated high-pressure processing, and ultra-short cycle times compared to traditional compression molding.

With over 30 years of manufacturing experience, Jun-Hsiang Enterprise outlines the top metrics for engineering and procurement teams evaluating custom Taiwan LSR suppliers.

2. Key Metric 1: Injection Equipment & Dosing Systems

LSR requires precise 1:1 dynamic mixing of A/B liquid components. A professional LSR supplier must feature:

• 1:1 Metering & Dosing Pumps for exact ratio control and bubble-free mixing.

• High-Pressure LIM Injection Machines (e.g. Chuan Lih Fa) offering multi-stage pressure control.

• Cold Runner Molds with Vacuum Venting to minimize material waste and eliminate micro-voids.

3. Key Metric 2: Cold Runner Molds Design Expertise

Because LSR has extremely low viscosity, precision tooling is critical to avoid flash:

• Mold Design evaluating parting line location, gate positions, and wall thickness uniformity.

• High-Precision Tooling Tolerances enabling flashless or low-flash molded parts direct from the cavity.

4. Key Metric 3: Multi-Material Overmolding Capabilities

Complex parts require silicone overmolding onto plastics (PA66, PC, PBT) or metals (stainless steel, aluminum):

• Self-Bonding LSR for primerless chemical bonding with engineering plastics.

• Primer Coating & Surface Treatment for durable rubber-to-metal bonding.

5. Key Metric 4: Quality & Regulatory Compliance

• ISO 9001 Certification.

• FDA 21 CFR 177.2600 & USP Class VI Compliance for medical and food-grade applications.
