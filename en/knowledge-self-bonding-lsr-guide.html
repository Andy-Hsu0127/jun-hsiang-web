Self-Bonding LSR Injection Molding Guide: Primerless Plastic Overmolding

Understanding selective bonding chemistry for primerless adhesion to polar plastic inserts with clean mold release

What is Self-Bonding Liquid Silicone Rubber (Self-Bonding LSR)?

In traditional silicone overmolding and insert molding processes, bonding soft silicone onto hard thermoplastic substrates (such as PC, PA, or PBT) requires applying a primer coating prior to molding. This primer stage adds labor costs, extra processing cycles, and risks quality inconsistencies if the coating thickness or curing humidity fluctuates.

Self-Bonding LSR (Self-Adhesive Liquid Silicone Rubber) eliminates the need for primer coatings. Formulated with reactive adhesion promoters, self-bonding LSR forms strong chemical bonds directly with polar thermoplastic substrates during high-temperature vulcanization, achieving cohesive failure (where silicone tears before the bond breaks).

Core Technology: Selective Bonding Chemistry & Mold Anti-Sticking

A critical engineering question is: "If self-bonding LSR adheres to plastic substrates, will it stick to the tool steel mold cavity?" The answer lies in Selective Bonding Mechanism:

  • Targeted Reaction with Polar Thermoplastics: Adhesion promoters in premium self-bonding LSR are engineered to react exclusively with polar functional groups found in engineering thermoplastics (such as PA66, PC, and PBT), remaining non-reactive to polished tool steel cavity surfaces.
  • Mold Surface Release Protection: To ensure reliable mass production and clean part ejection, Jun-Hsiang Enterprise applies specialized surface treatments to mold steel cavities.
  • Clear Material Specialization: Self-bonding LSR is dedicated to engineering plastic insert overmolding. For metal insert overmolding, Jun-Hsiang applies our proven traditional metal surface preparation and primer bonding process.

Advantages of Plastic Insert Overmolding

Combining plastic inserts with self-bonding LSR delivers major manufacturing advantages:

• Eliminating Primer Application & Baking: Pre-molded PA, PC, or PBT inserts are placed into the LSR mold cavity and directly overmolded with self-bonding silicone, eliminating manual priming and baking steps.

• Zero Primer Residue & Better Yields: Solves quality issues associated with inconsistent manual primer coverage or overspray, boosting final assembly yield.

• Medical & Food-Grade Compliance: Avoiding solvent-based primers eliminates VOC emissions and ensures compliance with FDA and medical biocompatibility standards.

Industrial & High-Tech Applications

Plastic insert overmolding with self-bonding LSR is widely adopted across demanding industries requiring High-grade waterproof sealing sealing and long-term durability:

• Automotive Electronics & Plastic Components: Automotive ECU plastic housing seals, sensor plastic base gaskets, and electrical connector plastic housing sealing strips (PA66/PBT plastic inserts overmolded with self-bonding LSR for High-grade waterproof sealing protection).

• Medical Devices & Healthcare Plastic Parts: Medical respiratory plastic connector overmolding and needleless injection valve plastic housing seals (free from primer chemical residue risks).

• Consumer Electronics & Industrial Plastic Enclosures: Industrial instrument plastic housing waterproof seals and wearable plastic structural gaskets.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Will Self-Bonding LSR stick to the steel mold during molding?

No. Premium self-bonding LSR utilizes selective bonding formulation that targets polar thermoplastics (PA, PC, PBT) while remaining unreactive to tool steel. Combined with Jun-Hsiang's mold surface treatments and zoned temperature control, clean mold release is ensured.

Can Self-Bonding LSR be used for metal overmolding?

Currently self-bonding silicone technology is specialized for polar thermoplastic substrates. For metal insert overmolding (such as stainless steel, aluminum, or copper), Jun-Hsiang Enterprise uses our proven traditional metal surface preparation and dedicated primer bonding process to achieve excellent adhesion strength.
